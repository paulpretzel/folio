# Folio privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

**The short version:** Folio doesn't collect anything about you. Your books and everything you do in Folio stay on your device. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nobody, including Folio's developer, can see what you read.

## What stays on your device

Everything you put into Folio is stored only on the device you use it on:

- the books you add, and the covers Folio makes for them
- the details Folio works out about each book (title, author, kind of book, suggested album)
- your albums, bookmarks, reading positions and reading time
- your settings

Folio works these out on the device itself. It never sends your books or their contents anywhere to be analysed.

## What Folio sends: nothing

Folio has no servers and doesn't send your data to anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party code that talks to the internet.

The app is locked down so its pages can't contact other servers. You can check this yourself: **Settings → Privacy** shows every connection Folio has made to a server other than its own, and every one it blocked.

Folio connects to another server only when you ask it to, for example if you download a free book from Folio's book list, or from a catalog you added yourself. Those connections go straight to that book's host, show up in Settings → Privacy, and send nothing about you beyond what any download does (your IP address, which the host sees).

## Files you open

Folio sees only the files you give it, through Add book, Share or Open with. It doesn't ask for access to all the files on your device.

## Backups

**Back up** creates a file with your albums, bookmarks and reading positions. It goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Folio never uploads it.

## The web version

If you use Folio as a web app, your browser downloads it, and later its updates, from the web address you installed it from. Like any website, that web host sees your IP address when the app loads or updates. Folio's developer doesn't use those logs to identify or track anyone.

## Purchases

If you buy Folio Pro in the Android app, Google Play handles the payment, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Folio only learns from Google Play whether Pro is unlocked on your device. It doesn't receive your name, email address or payment details.

## Children

Folio collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

## Deleting your data

Removing a book in Folio deletes it and its bookmarks from the device. Uninstalling Folio, or clearing its data, deletes everything Folio stored.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions go to the developer contact email shown on Folio's Google Play page.

Folio's source code is public at [github.com/paulpretzel/folio](https://github.com/paulpretzel/folio), so anyone can check these promises against the code.
